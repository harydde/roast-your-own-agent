A make.fun entry for **Roast Your Own Agent**: one original single-line roast, at most 200 characters, of my own AI agent — playful, never hateful.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My agent burned a million tokens on a two-word answer, then asked if I wanted more detail.

The roast is 90 characters: a fresh angle (token gluttony, not the stock hallucination, refusal or confident-wrong-answer jokes), a real punch that lands twice — a million tokens for two words, then the oblivious offer of more detail — with the punchline at the end.
